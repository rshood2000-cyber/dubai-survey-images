# Street imagery - MSc dissertation perception survey

Street-level imagery used in an online perception survey for an MSc
dissertation on pedestrian walking comfort in Dubai, University of Birmingham
Dubai.

The survey asks participants to rate how comfortable each street would feel to
walk along, overall and in terms of heat and shade. Ratings are used to train a
computer-vision model predicting perceived walking comfort from streetscape
imagery.
