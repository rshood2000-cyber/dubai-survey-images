# Street imagery — MSc dissertation perception survey

Street-level imagery used in an online perception survey for an MSc dissertation on pedestrian walking comfort in Dubai, University of Birmingham Dubai.

The final survey image pool contains **120 street-level images** drawn from four mixed-use study districts in Dubai:

- City Walk
- Al Karama
- Al Souq Al Kabeer
- Dubai Marina

Survey participants are shown a randomized subset of images and rate perceived overall walking comfort and thermal comfort.
